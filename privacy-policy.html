Ounce Video Compressor

Privacy Policy

Effective 28 October 2026

In short: Ounce does not collect, store or send any personal data. Your videos are processed on your own computer and never leave it.

Who we are

Ounce Video Compressor (“Ounce”, “the app”) is published by Anton Zhezherun (“we”, “us”). This policy covers the Windows app as distributed through the Microsoft Store and our website.

What the app does with your videos

  • All compression, analysis (including quality measurement) and preview frames are produced locally on your PC, using the copy of FFmpeg bundled with the app.
  • Your videos, their file names, their contents and their metadata are never uploaded, copied to us, or shared with anyone.
  • The app only reads the files you add to it and writes the compressed copies to the folder you choose. It does not modify or delete your originals.

Data we collect

None. The app has no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting and no telemetry. It makes no network connections of its own and works fully offline.

Data stored on your computer

The app saves its own settings (for example your preferred quality preset, codec and output folder) and a small note of which video encoders your graphics card supports, so it does not have to test them at every start, in %APPDATA%\Ounce on your PC. These files stay on your computer, are not sent anywhere, and are removed when you delete that folder. Uninstalling the app does not send us anything.

Purchases and the trial

Ounce Pro is sold through itch.io. “Get Pro” opens the itch.io page in your browser, and itch.io handles the payment and delivers your licence key under the itch.io Privacy Policy. As the seller we may see the order details itch.io provides for a purchase, such as the email address used. The app itself never handles payment details.

The app checks a licence key on your PC, with no network connection, and saves it in %APPDATA%\Ounce. The 7-day trial is recorded in that folder and in the Windows registry, sealed using your PC's Windows machine ID so that it cannot be restarted. Neither the key nor that ID is ever sent to us.

Children

Because the app collects no personal data, it collects none from children.

Changes to this policy

If a future version changes how data is handled, we will update this page and the date above before that version is released. We will not start collecting personal data without saying so here first.
